2. PURPOSE, TARGET AUDIENCE & VALUE
- Audience: University students, medical/engineering/law exam candidates, educators, and visual thinkers.
- Problem & Value: Linear notes are passive and hard to recall. ZihinHaritam (MindSticker) applies dual-coding memory principles by pairing color-coded Bezier mind maps with 25,200+ instant offline academic stickers, on-device diagram cropping into die-cut stickers, Apple Pencil sketches, Active Recall self-testing, and 300 DPI PDF/SVG exports.

3. SETUP & MAIN FEATURE INSTRUCTIONS
- Credentials / Sample Files: NONE needed; 5 academic mind maps are preloaded out of the box.
- Steps:
  1) Tap any template on Home Workspace (or "+ Boş Görsel Harita" for up to 3 free maps).
  2) Double-tap a node (or "+ Yeni Dal") and type a term ("kalp", "sunucu", "mahkeme", "atom") for instant offline stickers above the keyboard.
  3) Tap the graduation-cap icon (top HUD) for Active Recall Exam Mode; tap hidden nodes to reveal.
  4) Tap Share (top-right) for PNG/SVG/300 DPI PDF export, or Gear for Settings, .zihin backup, Legal docs, and Restore Purchases.
  5) Paid Content (com.tahafirat.zihinharitam.lifetime_pro, Non-Consumable one-time unlock): tap the "Kilidi Aç" Pro pill, any "PRO" sticker, or create a 4th map to test StoreKit 2 Sandbox purchase and "Satın Alımları Geri Yükle" (AppStore.sync()).
