ZihinHaritam v1.0.3 (Build 21) App Review Information:

1. No Account Required:
The app works 100% offline-first without login, signup, or external API keys. All mind maps, ink drawings, sticky notes, and icons are stored locally in on-device IndexedDB.

2. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (StoreKit 2):
- Monthly Pro (Auto-Renewable): com.tahafirat.zihinharitam.pro_monthly ($6.99/mo | ₺349.99/mo in Turkey)
- Yearly Pro (Auto-Renewable): com.tahafirat.zihinharitam.pro_yearly ($39.99/yr | ₺2,099.99/yr in Turkey)
- Lifetime Pro (Non-Consumable): com.tahafirat.zihinharitam.lifetime_pro ($69.99 | ₺3,499.99 in Turkey)
- Offer Codes on iOS use Apple's official StoreKit 2 AppStore.presentOfferCodeRedeemSheet API (no external code redemption or payment bypass).
- Strict SKU verification ensures the exact user-selected productId is requested from StoreKit 2.
- "Restore Purchases" is accessible in both the Paywall modal and Settings -> License & Backup.

3. Free vs Pro Quotas:
- Free users can create up to 2 active mind maps (with permanent delete confirmation; 30-day Trash recovery is Pro-only) and browse 16,000+ free icons + full drawing tools.
- Pro unlocks unlimited maps, 30-day Trash Can, 43,000+ vibrant gamified & 3D icons, Full Exam Mode, and 300 DPI PDF/SVG export.

4. Apple Pencil & Tablet Features:
- Tap "Çiz" (Draw) in the top bar to open the draggable, edge-snapping floating drawing toolbar (Pen, Highlighter, Laser Pointer, Lasso, Stroke/Area Eraser, Shapes, Auto-Shape, Sticky Notes, Paper Templates, Palm Rejection).
- Apple Pencil 2nd Gen double-tap and Apple Pencil Pro squeeze toggle Pen/Eraser cleanly via UIPencilInteractionDelegate.
